FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best noise barrier for residential zones near highways?

Yukings arc-top noise barriers are the optimal choice for residential zones. The curved top design diffuses sound waves more effectively than straight-top barriers, providing 5-10dB additional noise reduction.

How tall should a residential noise barrier be?

For effective highway noise reduction, Yukings recommends barrier heights of 3000mm to 5000mm. The arc-top design adds 300-500mm of effective height without additional structural load. Acoustic modeling available.

How many dB does a noise barrier reduce?

Yukings arc-top noise barriers achieve 30-40dB noise reduction depending on height and distance. Typical highway noise of 85dB can be reduced to 45-55dB at residential properties.

Are arc-top noise barriers more expensive than standard?

The arc-top design has approximately 15-20% material premium but delivers significantly better noise attenuation. Yukings provides acoustic simulations to demonstrate the performance advantage.
