FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a school campus noise barrier?

A school campus noise barrier is a child-safe acoustic wall designed for school perimeters and playgrounds. Yukings school barriers deliver 25-35dB reduction with R10 rounded edges and zero-VOC non-toxic coating.

Are school barriers safe for children?

Yes! Yukings school barriers use R10 minimum rounded edge radius eliminating sharp edges and pinch points. Tamper-resistant fasteners prevent unauthorized disassembly. EN 71-3 toy safety certified.

Are school barrier coatings non-toxic?

Yes! Yukings school barriers use zero-VOC powder coating containing no volatile organic compounds. Certified safe for children's respiratory health and compliant with EN 71-3 toy safety standard.

How much noise do school barriers reduce?

Yukings school campus barriers achieve 25-35dB reduction, sufficient to bring 75-85dB drop-off and pick-up noise down to 40-60dB at classroom windows, improving reading comprehension and concentration.

Can school barriers use school colors?

Yes! Yukings school barriers support custom RAL color matching to school identity, including primary color schemes, team colors, and integrated school logos and mascots in panel design.

What is the minimum order quantity for school barriers?

Yukings standard MOQ is 100㎡ for school campus noise barriers. Custom school colors or logos may require 200㎡ minimum order quantity.
