FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best noise barrier for high wind areas?

Yukings louver noise barriers are engineered for high wind conditions. Louvered panels reduce wind load by up to 40% compared to solid panels while maintaining 25-35dB noise reduction.

How does the louver design reduce noise?

Yukings louver panels feature angled slots that break up sound waves while allowing air passage. The panel cavities contain mineral wool absorption material that converts sound energy to heat.

What sizes do louver noise barrier panels come in?

Standard panel widths are 2000mm and 2490mm. Heights range from 500mm to 4000mm. Panel thickness: 80mm standard, 100mm for enhanced noise reduction. Custom panel sizes available.

Do louver barriers require special maintenance?

No special maintenance is required. Yukings galvanized steel panels resist corrosion for 25+ years. Louver openings prevent debris buildup.
