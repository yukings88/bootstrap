FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is an arc top highway noise barrier?

Arc top design features a curved upper edge (radius R500-R3000) with a transparent PC top panel. The curved profile reduces edge diffraction 3-5dB vs flat barriers while preserving scenic views through clear PC panels.

How effective is the PC transparent top panel?

Yukings PC top panels deliver 80%+ light transmission with 25-35dB noise reduction. UV-stabilized for 10-year non-yellowing warranty, ideal for scenic and tourism highway corridors.

Are arc top barriers suitable for landmark projects?

Yes! Arc top barriers are specifically designed for landmark, heritage, and tourism-corridor highway projects. Custom RAL colors and arc radii match any architectural specification, with full BIM support.

What is the minimum order quantity for arc top barriers?

Yukings standard MOQ is 100㎡ for arc top highway noise barriers. Custom radii or PC panel configurations may require 200㎡ MOQ. Landmark projects enjoy flexible terms.

How long does the PC top panel last?

UV-stabilized PC panels carry 10-year non-yellowing warranty. Full service life 25-30 years with proper installation. Yellowing prevention through co-extruded UV protective layer.

Can arc top barriers be customized for specific architectural styles?

Yes! Custom arc radii from R500 to R3000+, full RAL color palette, and PC panel tints (clear, smoked, opaque). BIM integration and 3D modeling available for landmark project specifications.
