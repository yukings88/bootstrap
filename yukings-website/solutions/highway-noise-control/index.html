Complete Highway Acoustic Solutions

Yukings provides comprehensive highway noise control solutions for expressways, urban arterial roads, bridge infrastructure and toll plaza areas. Our engineering team delivers site-specific acoustic assessment, traffic noise simulation and custom highway noise barrier design to comply with environmental noise regulations.

Achieving up to 45dB noise reduction, our acoustic barriers effectively mitigate vehicle engine noise, tire-road interaction noise and aerodynamic noise from high-speed traffic. All systems meet ISO9001, CE certification and international highway standards for durability and acoustic performance.
