Complete Railway Acoustic Solutions

Yukings delivers comprehensive railway noise reduction solutions for high-speed rail networks, urban metro systems, freight corridors and light rail transit. Our engineering team provides site-specific acoustic assessment, simulation modeling and custom railway noise barrier design to meet stringent environmental noise standards.

With up to 45dB noise reduction performance, our acoustic barriers effectively mitigate wheel-rail noise, aerodynamic noise and structural vibration transmission. All systems are certified to ISO9001, CE and international railway standards.

Solution Advantages

Our railway noise reduction solutions are engineered for maximum acoustic performance, durability and rapid deployment

Up to 45dB Noise Reduction

Industrial-grade acoustic panels laboratory tested for railway noise barrier applications, delivering maximum 45dB sound insulation for high-speed rail and metro environments.

IP65-IP66 Protection Rating

Waterproof and corrosion resistant barriers with 2000-hour salt spray test certification, engineered for railway environments with temperature range -40°C to +80°C.

Modular Fast Installation

Standard modular panels with bolt connection structure enable rapid trackside installation, minimizing railway operation disruption and reducing construction timeline.

Custom Engineering Design

Complete acoustic simulation, CAD design, color and non-standard size customization for railway-specific requirements including track curvature and platform integration.
