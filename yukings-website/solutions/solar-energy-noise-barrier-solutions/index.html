Complete Solar Acoustic Solutions

Yukings delivers innovative solar noise barrier solutions that integrate photovoltaic modules with acoustic panels for dual-function infrastructure. Our engineering team provides comprehensive solar acoustic design, energy yield optimization and custom solar acoustic barrier engineering for highways, railways and industrial facilities.

Achieving up to 45dB noise reduction while generating clean electricity, our solar acoustic barriers transform noise control infrastructure into renewable energy assets. All systems are certified to ISO9001, CE and international photovoltaic standards for sustainable infrastructure development.

Solution Advantages

Our solar noise barrier solutions deliver dual benefits of acoustic performance and renewable energy generation for sustainable infrastructure

Up to 45dB Noise Reduction

Industrial-grade acoustic panels laboratory tested for solar noise barrier applications, delivering maximum 45dB sound insulation while maintaining photovoltaic module efficiency.

IP65-IP66 Protection Rating

Waterproof and corrosion resistant barriers with 2000-hour salt spray test certification, engineered for outdoor solar installations with temperature range -40°C to +80°C.

Modular Fast Installation

Standard modular panels with integrated photovoltaic mounting system enable rapid installation, combining acoustic assembly with solar module deployment.

Custom Engineering Design

Complete acoustic simulation, solar yield optimization, CAD design and electrical integration for solar-specific requirements including grid connection and energy management systems.
