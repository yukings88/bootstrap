Residential Noise Barrier Aesthetic Design

Our residential noise barriers combine acoustic performance with aesthetic appeal for community applications. The panel surfaces feature customizable finishes including RAL color options, wood grain textures, and stone patterns, blending seamlessly with residential landscapes while achieving 45dB noise reduction performance.

The lightweight panel structure includes UV-resistant surface treatments with ≥2000 hours testing certification, ensuring color stability under prolonged sun exposure. Material options include galvanized steel, aluminum, PC transparent panels, and composite materials for various aesthetic requirements.
