Industrial Noise Barrier Heavy-Duty Structure

Our industrial noise barriers feature reinforced sandwich panel construction designed for harsh factory environments. The outer layers utilize galvanized steel or aluminum with acid/alkali resistant coatings, while the core contains high-density acoustic insulation achieving 45dB noise reduction for machinery and equipment noise.

The structural design includes heavy-duty H-post columns engineered for ≥4.5 kN/m² wind load resistance and Class A fire rating for industrial safety compliance. Surface treatments undergo 2000-hour salt spray test certification for chemical corrosion resistance.

Industrial Facility Installation Layout

The industrial noise barrier installation accommodates flexible configurations around factory equipment, facility boundaries, and perimeter fencing. Modular panels can be arranged in curved or angled layouts to match specific plant layouts, with heights ranging from 500mm to 5000mm for various industrial noise control requirements.

Installation details include foundation anchoring systems, vibration isolation mounts, and maintenance access panels. The IP65-IP66 protection rating ensures reliable operation in industrial environments with dust, humidity, and chemical exposure conditions.
