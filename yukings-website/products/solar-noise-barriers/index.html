Innovative Photovoltaic Acoustic Barrier Solutions

Yukings solar noise barriers represent cutting-edge sustainable infrastructure technology, combining effective noise reduction with solar power generation. These innovative photovoltaic acoustic barriers serve dual purposes: protecting communities from traffic noise while generating clean renewable energy.

Our solar sound barriers integrate high-efficiency PV modules into acoustic panel structures, achieving up to 45dB noise reduction while producing electricity. Ideal for highway corridors, railway lines, and industrial zones where noise mitigation and energy generation are both priorities.

With 109 independent patents and HIT R&D team support, Yukings provides custom solar acoustic barrier engineering including PV module integration design, electrical system configuration, and acoustic optimization. Products exported to Germany, USA, Australia, and Southeast Asia for green infrastructure projects.
