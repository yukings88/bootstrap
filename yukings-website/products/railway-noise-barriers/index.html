Railway Noise Barrier Cross-Section Structure

Our railway acoustic barriers feature a multi-layer sandwich structure design. The outer panels are made of galvanized steel or aluminum with perforated sound-absorbing surfaces, while the core layer contains high-density acoustic insulation material for maximum noise reduction performance.

The modular panel system includes H-post steel columns for structural support, with bolt connection mechanisms enabling rapid assembly. Each panel module is designed for 45dB noise reduction capability, tested and verified in our factory laboratory.

Modular Installation System

The railway noise barrier installation utilizes a modular bolt-connection system, enabling rapid on-site assembly without specialized equipment. Standard panel modules fit precisely into H-post column slots, reducing construction time by up to 40% compared to traditional methods.

Each connection point features corrosion-resistant hardware with 2000-hour salt spray test certification, ensuring long-term durability in railway environments. The modular design also facilitates easy maintenance access and panel replacement when needed.
