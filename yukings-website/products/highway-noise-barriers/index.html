Professional Highway Sound Barrier Solutions

Yukings highway noise barriers are engineered for expressways, highways, urban roads, and major transportation corridors. Our sound walls effectively reduce traffic noise pollution, protecting nearby residential communities and commercial areas from excessive road noise.

All highway acoustic barriers feature weather-resistant construction with IP65-IP66 protection rating, ensuring long-term durability against rain, wind, and UV exposure. Laboratory testing confirms up to 45dB noise reduction performance for highway applications.

Highway Sound Barrier Cross-Section Design

Our highway noise barriers utilize a robust sandwich panel structure with weather-resistant outer layers. The perforated metal surface absorbs sound waves while the inner acoustic insulation core provides maximum 45dB noise reduction performance for highway traffic noise.

The structural design includes reinforced H-post columns engineered for ≥4.0 kN/m² wind load resistance, ensuring stability along open highway corridors. Surface treatments feature UV-resistant coatings with ≥2000 hours testing certification.

Rapid Highway Installation Process

The highway sound barrier modular system enables rapid installation with minimal traffic disruption. Standard 1960mm wide panels connect via bolt mechanisms to H-post columns, allowing assembly rates of up to 50 meters per day with trained installation teams.

Installation details include pre-drilled connection points, leveling adjustments, and foundation anchoring systems. The IP65-IP66 protection rating ensures all connection hardware withstands highway environmental conditions including rain, wind, and UV exposure.
