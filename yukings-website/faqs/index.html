Ordering & Pricing

1 What is the minimum order quantity (MOQ) for noise barriers?

Yukings offers flexible minimum order quantities to accommodate various project sizes. For standard highway noise barriers and railway acoustic barriers, the MOQ starts at 100 square meters. For smaller residential or pilot projects, we can accommodate orders of 50 square meters. Bulk orders exceeding 1000 square meters receive preferential wholesale pricing. Contact our sales team for specific MOQ details based on your project requirements.

Related: Request Quote | View Products

2 How is the pricing determined for noise barrier projects?

Pricing depends on several factors: material type (galvanized steel, aluminum, transparent PC, concrete), panel dimensions, acoustic performance requirements, quantity, and customization level. Our acoustic barriers range from $50-150 per square meter depending on specifications. Standard products with 20-working-day delivery offer competitive pricing, while custom industrial noise barrier solutions with acoustic simulation and CAD design may have higher costs. We provide free detailed quotations within 24 hours.

3 Do you offer wholesale pricing for large-scale projects?

Yes, Yukings provides tiered wholesale pricing for bulk orders. Projects exceeding 500 square meters receive 5-10% discount, while orders over 2000 square meters qualify for 15-20% volume pricing. As a direct manufacturer with own production facility, we eliminate middleman costs, offering competitive factory-direct prices for highway noise barrier projects and infrastructure developments. Long-term partnership agreements unlock additional pricing benefits.

Product Specifications & Performance

4 What noise reduction performance can I expect from your barriers?

Yukings noise barriers achieve up to 45dB noise reduction as verified by factory laboratory testing. Standard galvanized steel panels typically deliver 35-45dB reduction depending on configuration. Aluminum barriers achieve 40-43dB, while transparent PC panels provide 35-40dB. Our solar noise barriers maintain full acoustic performance while generating electricity. All products undergo third-party acoustic testing with documented results.

Related: Acoustic Testing Reports | Technical Articles

5 What certifications and quality standards do your products meet?

Yukings products hold comprehensive certifications: ISO 9001/14001/45001 quality, environmental, and occupational health management systems, CE EU export certification, third-party acoustic testing qualifications, and compliance with ASTM American standards, DIN German standards, and GB Chinese national standards. Our manufacturing facility maintains strict quality control processes, ensuring industrial sound barriers meet international requirements for USA, Canada, Germany, UK, Australia, and Southeast Asia markets.

Related: View Certifications | Factory Tour

6 What is the lifespan and warranty for your noise barriers?

Yukings acoustic barriers feature 25-year design lifespan with 15-year structural warranty. Products undergo 2000-hour salt spray tests ensuring corrosion resistance for harsh environments. IP65-IP66 waterproof ratings protect against weather exposure. Operating temperature range spans -40°C to +80°C, suitable for diverse climates from Canadian winters to Middle Eastern summers. Our residential noise barriers and commercial installations maintain performance throughout the warranty period.

Installation & Support

12 How difficult is the installation process for your noise barriers?

Our modular noise barriers feature fast installation with bolt connection structures. Standard panels connect quickly between H-posts or I-posts, significantly reducing on-site construction time compared to traditional systems. Installation requires basic construction equipment and standard tools. We provide detailed installation guides, CAD drawings, and technical support. For highway noise control projects, installation typically progresses at 50-100 meters per day with experienced crews.

Yes, Yukings provides comprehensive installation support: detailed installation manuals and video guides, foundation design specifications, on-site technical consultation for large projects, remote engineering support via WhatsApp and email, and post-installation performance verification guidance. 14 What maintenance is required for noise barriers?

Our acoustic barriers require minimal maintenance due to IP65-IP66 waterproof ratings and corrosion resistance. Recommended maintenance includes: annual visual inspection for damage or wear, periodic cleaning to remove debris and dirt, checking bolt connections for tightness, and addressing any impact damage promptly. Products passing 2000-hour salt spray tests demonstrate exceptional durability. With proper maintenance, residential sound barriers and commercial installations maintain performance throughout the 25-year design lifespan.
