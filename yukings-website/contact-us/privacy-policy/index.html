Privacy Policy

GDPR compliant data protection policy for Yukings Noise Barrier Manufacturer website visitors and customers

Home / Contact Us / Privacy Policy

1. Introduction

Yukings Noise Barrier Manufacturer ("Yukings", "we", "us", or "our") operates the website www.yukings.net and provides professional noise barrier manufacturing and acoustic engineering services. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your personal information when you visit our website or engage with our services.

We are committed to protecting your privacy and complying with applicable data protection laws, including the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) for European Union visitors, and other relevant privacy regulations for global markets including USA, Canada, Germany, UK, Australia, and Southeast Asia.

Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. By using our website or services, you consent to the practices described in this policy.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Personal Information

We may collect personal information that you voluntarily provide when you:

  • Submit an inquiry form or quotation request
  • Contact us via email, telephone, or WhatsApp
  • Register for newsletters or updates
  • Download technical documents or product specifications
  • Participate in surveys or feedback requests

Personal information may include:

  • Name and contact details (email address, phone number, WhatsApp)
  • Company name and business information
  • Country and geographic location
  • Project requirements and specifications
  • Technical drawings or documents you share with us

2.2 Automatically Collected Information

When you visit our website, we may automatically collect certain information using cookies and similar technologies:

  • Browser type and version
  • Operating system
  • IP address and geographic location
  • Pages visited and time spent on pages
  • Referring website addresses
  • Device information and screen resolution

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

  • Process and respond to your inquiries and quotation requests
  • Provide customer support and technical assistance
  • Send product information, updates, and promotional materials (with your consent)
  • Improve our website functionality and user experience
  • Analyze website traffic and usage patterns
  • Comply with legal obligations and regulations
  • Protect against fraudulent activities and security threats

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for marketing purposes without your explicit consent.

4. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

Our website uses cookies and similar tracking technologies to enhance your browsing experience and collect usage data. Cookies are small data files stored on your device that help us:

  • Remember your preferences and settings
  • Analyze website traffic and performance
  • Provide personalized content and recommendations
  • Ensure website security and functionality

4.1 Types of Cookies We Use

Essential Cookies: Required for basic website functionality, such as page navigation and form submission. These cookies cannot be disabled.

Analytics Cookies: Used to collect anonymous statistics about website usage, helping us improve our services and content.

Functional Cookies: Remember your preferences and settings to provide enhanced user experience.

Marketing Cookies: Used to deliver relevant advertisements and track campaign effectiveness (only with your consent).

4.2 Managing Cookies

You can manage cookie preferences through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to block or delete cookies. However, disabling certain cookies may affect website functionality. Our website displays a cookie consent banner allowing you to accept or decline non-essential cookies.

5. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We may share your personal information with:

  • Service Providers: Third-party companies that assist us in website hosting, email delivery, analytics, and customer support. These providers are bound by confidentiality agreements.
  • Business Partners: Authorized partners involved in project delivery, such as logistics providers for product shipping.
  • Legal Authorities: When required by law, regulation, or legal process to protect our rights or comply with government requests.
  • Corporate Transactions: In connection with mergers, acquisitions, or business transfers, with appropriate confidentiality protections.

We do not share your personal information with unauthorized third parties or for purposes unrelated to our business operations.

6. Data Security and Storage

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction:

  • Secure data encryption for sensitive information transmission
  • Access controls and authentication for data systems
  • Regular security assessments and vulnerability testing
  • Employee training on data protection practices
  • Backup and recovery systems for data preservation

Your personal information is stored on secure servers located in China, with appropriate safeguards for international data transfers where applicable. We retain personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy, unless longer retention is required by law.

7. Your Rights Under GDPR

If you are located in the European Union, you have the following rights under GDPR:

  • Right to Access: Request a copy of the personal information we hold about you.
  • Right to Rectification: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete personal information.
  • Right to Erasure: Request deletion of your personal information in certain circumstances.
  • Right to Restriction: Request limitation of processing your personal information.
  • Right to Data Portability: Request transfer of your personal information to another service provider.
  • Right to Object: Object to processing of your personal information for certain purposes.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: Withdraw consent for processing based on consent at any time.

To exercise these rights, please contact us using the contact information provided below. We will respond to your request within 30 days in accordance with GDPR requirements.

8. International Data Transfers

As a global noise barrier manufacturer serving markets in USA, Canada, Germany, UK, Australia, and Southeast Asia, we may transfer personal information across international borders. We ensure appropriate safeguards for such transfers:

  • Standard contractual clauses approved by relevant authorities
  • Binding corporate rules for internal data transfers
  • Explicit consent for specific transfer purposes
  • Compliance with applicable data protection laws in destination countries

For EU visitors, we ensure GDPR-compliant mechanisms for any transfers outside the European Union.

9. Children's Privacy

Our website and services are intended for business customers and professional users. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 16 years of age. If you believe we have inadvertently collected information from a child, please contact us immediately so we can take appropriate action to delete such information.

10. Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to third-party websites, including social media platforms (LinkedIn, Facebook, YouTube, Instagram) and external resources. These third-party websites have their own privacy policies, and we are not responsible for their data practices. We encourage you to review the privacy policies of any third-party websites you visit.

11. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in our practices, legal requirements, or operational needs. The updated policy will be posted on this page with the "Last Updated" date indicated below. We encourage you to review this policy regularly to stay informed about our data protection practices.
