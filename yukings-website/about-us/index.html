About Yukings

Yukings is a professional noise barrier manufacturer with independent production facilities, specializing in acoustic barrier panels and配套 equipment. We support bulk wholesale pricing and provide free engineering noise assessment and project quotation within 24 hours.

Located in Shenzhen, Guangdong, China, Yukings has established itself as a leading supplier of noise barriers and acoustic solutions for railway, highway, industrial, and residential applications worldwide.

With 109 independent patents supported by HIT R&D team, we deliver innovative noise reduction barriers that meet international standards including ISO 9001/14001/45001 and CE certifications.
