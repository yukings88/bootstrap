Yukings Manufacturing Facilities

Yukings operates a state-of-the-art manufacturing facility in Shenzhen, Guangdong, China, equipped with advanced production lines for noise barrier panels and acoustic barrier systems. Our factory covers over 20,000 square meters with dedicated workshops for metal processing, acoustic panel assembly, and quality testing.

With annual production capacity exceeding 500,000 square meters of sound barriers, we serve global markets including USA, Canada, Germany, UK, Australia, and Southeast Asia. Our modular production system enables fast lead time: 20 working days for standard items and 40 days for custom orders.

Every noise reduction barrier undergoes rigorous quality control including acoustic performance testing, salt spray corrosion testing, and structural integrity verification in our in-house laboratory.

Production Workshop Capabilities

01

Metal Processing Workshop

Advanced CNC cutting, bending, and welding equipment for galvanized steel and aluminum noise barrier panels. Precision manufacturing ensures consistent quality for highway and railway acoustic barriers.

  • CNC laser cutting machines
  • Automatic bending systems
  • Robotic welding stations
  • Precision stamping equipment
02

Acoustic Panel Assembly

Specialized assembly lines for acoustic panel production with sound-absorbing materials integration. Modular design enables fast installation and IP65-IP66 waterproof performance.

  • Acoustic material filling systems
  • Panel sealing equipment
  • Surface coating lines
  • Quality inspection stations
03

Quality Testing Laboratory

In-house laboratory for acoustic performance testing, corrosion resistance verification, and structural integrity analysis. All products tested to achieve up to 45dB noise reduction.

  • Acoustic performance testing chamber
  • 2000-hour salt spray test equipment
  • Load bearing testing systems
  • Environmental simulation chambers
04

Surface Treatment Workshop

Advanced coating and surface treatment facilities ensuring IP65-IP66 waterproof and corrosion resistance. Powder coating and hot-dip galvanizing for durable outdoor performance.

  • Automatic powder coating lines
  • Hot-dip galvanizing equipment
  • Color customization systems
  • UV-resistant coating application
