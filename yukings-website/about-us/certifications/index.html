Quality Assurance at Yukings

Yukings maintains comprehensive quality management systems to ensure every noise barrier product meets international standards. Our certifications demonstrate commitment to quality, environmental responsibility, and occupational safety for global markets including USA, Canada, Germany, UK, Australia, and Southeast Asia.

All acoustic barrier products undergo rigorous testing in our in-house laboratory and third-party certification bodies, achieving up to 45dB noise reduction performance. Our sound barriers comply with GB Chinese standards, ASTM American standards, and DIN German standards for diverse international project requirements.

With 15-year structural warranty and 25-year design lifespan, Yukings noise reduction barriers deliver reliable long-term performance for railway, highway, industrial, and residential applications worldwide.
