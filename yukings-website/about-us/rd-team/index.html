HIT Research Partnership

Yukings maintains a strategic R&D partnership with Harbin Institute of Technology (HIT), one of China's leading engineering universities. This collaboration enables us to develop advanced noise barrier technologies and acoustic engineering solutions that meet international standards for USA, Canada, Germany, UK, Australia, and Southeast Asia markets.

Our joint R&D team has achieved 109 independent patents in noise barrier technology, covering acoustic panel design, modular installation systems, corrosion-resistant materials, and sound absorption optimization. These innovations enable us to deliver acoustic barriers with up to 45dB noise reduction performance.

The HIT partnership provides Yukings with access to advanced acoustic simulation modeling, CAD design capabilities, and material science research, ensuring our sound barriers achieve optimal performance for railway, highway, industrial, and residential applications worldwide.
