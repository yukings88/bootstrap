Installation Process

High-Speed Rail Noise Barrier: Easy Installation & Maintenance

Specialized rail installation crew with bolted post-base system, no on-site welding required for fatigue-critical joints. Detailed installation manual per EN 16272.

1
Step 01

Foundation Work

Pour reinforced concrete foundations at 2m intervals per rail engineering drawing. Anchor bolt set to ±0.5mm tolerance.

2
Step 02

Post-Base Bolting

Bolt H-beam posts to anchor plates with high-strength friction-grip bolts. No on-site welding.

3
Step 03

Isolator & Panel Mounting

Mount spring isolators on posts, then slide acoustic panels into place. Lock with security fasteners.

4
Step 04

Inspection & Certification

Perform aerodynamic, fatigue, and acoustic testing per EN 16272. Submit certification documentation.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a high-speed rail noise barrier?

A high-speed rail noise barrier is an aerodynamic acoustic wall designed for 300-350km/h high-speed rail lines. Yukings HSR barriers deliver 30-40dB reduction with anti-vibration design fatigue-tested to 2M+ cycles per EN 16272.

How much noise do HSR barriers reduce?

Yukings HSR barriers achieve 30-40dB reduction, sufficient to bring 90-105dB HSR aerodynamic noise down to 50-65dB at residential properties within 100m of rail lines.

Are HSR barriers EN 16272 compliant?

Yes! All Yukings HSR barriers are fully EN 16272 compliant for European high-speed rail projects. Acoustic, aerodynamic, and fatigue performance verified by accredited laboratories.

What is the minimum order quantity for HSR barriers?

Yukings standard MOQ is 200㎡ for HSR noise barriers. Rail corridor projects typically 5km+. Custom streamlining or branding may require 500㎡ minimum order quantity.

How long do HSR barriers last?

Yukings HSR barriers are designed for 30+ year service life, verified through 2,000,000+ cycle fatigue testing per EN 16272. Standard highway barriers last 5-7 years in the same HSR environment.

Can HSR barriers be installed on rail bridges?

Yes! Yukings HSR barriers are designed for ground-level and elevated bridge installation. Spring isolator mounts prevent vibration transfer to bridge structure.
