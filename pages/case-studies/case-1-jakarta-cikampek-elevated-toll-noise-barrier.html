Project Overview

PT Jasa Marga (Persero) Tbk commissioned Yukings to deliver an integrated noise-mitigation package along the elevated section of the Jakarta-Cikampek Toll Road, where 24-hour traffic volumes exceed 180,000 vehicles per day. The project was driven by a 2022 ministerial decree requiring a 25 dB(A) minimum insertion loss for all new elevated expressways passing within 50 m of residential zones.

Yukings supplied 1,068 noise barrier panels in 9 containerised shipments over 45 days, including all HEB 160 hot-rolled steel posts, base plates, anti-vibration EPDM gaskets, and PC transparent inserts. On-site installation was completed by a 14-person crew in 42 calendar days, fully coordinated with the main contractor's slab-pour schedule.

  • 3,200 m (10,499 ft) total barrier length on a 17 m elevated deck
  • 28 dB(A) measured insertion loss at the closest residential receiver
  • CE, ISO 9001, SNI 1729:2019 and ASTM A123 compliance delivered
  • 45-day door-to-door lead time including sea freight Shenzhen → Jakarta

Engineering

Project Challenge & Custom Solution

Yukings engineered a hybrid steel + transparent-panel barrier to balance acoustic performance with anti-glare requirements and natural-light preservation for the residential towers on the east elevation.

⚠

On-site Challenges

Tropical coastal humidity, 90 km/h typhoon wind gusts, and a 1.8% longitudinal deck slope demanded a structurally over-specified post system, while residents required unobstructed daylight and a panoramic view of the city skyline.

  • Noise source: Mixed traffic at 78 dB(A) daytime, 72 dB(A) night-time; dominant 250–1000 Hz tyre/road band
  • Site condition: Coastal-marine atmosphere, C4 corrosion class, 1.8% deck slope, soft alluvial subsoil
  • Compliance target: SNI 1729:2019 and Ministerial Decree No. 14/2022; EN 1793-4 acoustic long-term
  • Aesthetic requirement: 60% PC transparent ratio, RAL 7035 light grey posts, anti-glare matte finish
✓

Yukings Custom Solution

We designed a post-and-panel barrier using HEB 160 hot-rolled steel posts at 3.0 m centres, faced with 80 mm composite panels combining 0.75 mm galvanised skin, 64 kg/m³ rock-wool infill and 8 mm UV-stable PC transparent inserts.

  • Material: Galvanised steel (HDG 85 μm) + 8 mm Lexan PC transparent
  • Structure: HEB 160 H-post, base-plate anchored, designed to 2.5 kPa wind load
  • Acoustic infill: Rock wool, 64 kg/m³ density, 80 mm nominal thickness
  • Anti-corrosion: Hot-dip galvanising 85 μm + RAL 7035 polyester powder coating 60 μm
