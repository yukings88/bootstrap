Product Features

Acoustic Principle & Structure

Advanced sound absorption and insulation technology. Modular design for easy installation and long-term performance.

🛡️

Hot-Dip Galvanized Steel

Q235B steel substrate with Z275 zinc coating (275g/㎡) provides superior corrosion resistance. Withstands 25+ years in outdoor environments without structural degradation.

🔇

Perforated Absorptive Panel

60% perforation ratio with mineral wool core absorbs 95% of incident sound energy. NRC rating 0.85 ensures excellent noise reduction.

⚙️

Modular Connection System

Interlocking H-shaped posts with rubber gaskets create weather-tight seals. Installation tolerance ±2mm ensures consistent acoustic performance.

Installation Process

Easy Installation & Maintenance

Simple bolt-on installation with detailed guides. Minimal maintenance required, 25-year warranty on materials.

1

Foundation Work

Pour concrete foundations at 2m intervals per engineering specs. Cure for 7 days minimum before load application.

2

Post Installation

Mount galvanized H-beam posts with vertical alignment tolerance ±1mm. Secure with anchor bolts.

3

Panel Mounting

Insert panels between posts using rubber isolation mounts. Lock with security fasteners.

4

Sealing & Inspection

Apply weather sealant at all joints. Final inspection verifies alignment, anchorage, and acoustic seals.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best material for highway noise barrier?

Galvanized steel is the most popular choice for highway noise barriers due to its durability and cost-effectiveness. Yukings uses hot-dip galvanized steel with Z275 coating, providing 25+ years of corrosion resistance.

How effective are galvanized steel noise barriers?

Yukings galvanized steel noise barriers achieve 25-35dB noise reduction, effectively reducing traffic noise from 85dB to a comfortable 50-60dB. The perforated panel design with mineral wool core ensures excellent sound absorption performance.

What is the minimum order quantity for noise barriers?

Yukings standard MOQ is 100㎡ for standard galvanized steel highway barriers. For custom specifications, MOQ may be 200㎡. Flexible terms are available for first-time buyers.

How long does noise barrier installation take?

Installation typically takes 1-2 days per kilometer depending on terrain and barrier height. Yukings provides detailed installation guidelines, video tutorials, and optional on-site technical support.

What certifications do noise barriers need?

Yukings noise barriers are CE certified (EN 14388) and ISO 9001:2015 quality certified. Wind tunnel tested to Grade 8 (960Pa) and acoustic performance verified by accredited laboratories.
