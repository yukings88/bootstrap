FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a construction site temporary noise barrier?

A construction site temporary noise barrier is a portable, reusable acoustic wall designed for active construction zones. Yukings temporary barriers deliver 20-30dB reduction with 100+ reuse cycles and zero-foundation installation.

How much noise do construction site barriers reduce?

Yukings construction site temporary barriers achieve 20-30dB reduction, sufficient to bring 90-100dB construction noise down to 60-80dB at the property line, meeting most municipal noise ordinances.

How many times can construction site barriers be reused?

Yukings construction site temporary barriers are verified to 100+ reuse cycles without acoustic or structural degradation. Typical project life is 10-15 years of regular reuse.

How long does installation take?

Yukings construction site temporary barriers install in 30 minutes per panel with a 2-person crew. No foundation, no concrete cure time, no heavy equipment, no permits for portable units.

What is the minimum order quantity for construction site barriers?

Yukings standard MOQ is 50 panels for construction site temporary noise barriers. Smaller projects negotiable with rental option available.

Can construction site barriers be relocated during the project?

Yes! Yukings offers wheel-mounted bases for daily relocation or quick-release ballast for relocation every few days. Same panels, same performance, no degradation.
