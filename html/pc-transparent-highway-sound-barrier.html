Frequently Asked Questions

What is the light transmission rate of PC transparent barriers?

Our PC transparent highway sound barrier offers 70-85% light transmission, maintaining excellent visibility while reducing noise. Perfect for urban areas where preserving views is important alongside galvanized steel noise barriers for comprehensive solutions.

Are PC barriers suitable for urban landscape projects?

Absolutely! PC transparent barriers are ideal for urban areas, preserving views and enhancing aesthetic appeal. They work beautifully with residential noise reduction solutions in city environments across USA, Canada, Germany.

What about impact resistance?

PC material is 250 times stronger than glass, providing excellent impact resistance for highway applications. Our polycarbonate acoustic barrier can withstand flying debris and extreme weather conditions, making it suitable for both urban and industrial noise control environments.

How do I maintain PC transparent panels?

Regular cleaning with mild soap and water is recommended. Avoid abrasive cleaners. Our team provides maintenance guidelines for all our acoustic products, including highway noise barrier projects.
