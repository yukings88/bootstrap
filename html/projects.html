Project FAQ

Frequently Asked Project Questions

Practical answers to the questions our noise barrier project clients ask before signing a purchase order.

Can Yukings share reference noise barrier projects in my country?
Yes. Yukings has delivered noise barrier projects in 60+ countries including the USA, Germany, Australia, UAE, Indonesia, Saudi Arabia, Canada and the UK. Our sales manager Miss Tang can share case studies, test reports and client references that match your region on request, with non-disclosure agreements where required.
What is the typical delivery time for a bulk noise barrier order?
Standard sound barrier panels ship within 15-25 working days after drawing approval. A full 40HQ container load of galvanized steel or aluminum noise barriers is usually delivered 25-35 days to most major seaports. Expedited production slots are available for emergency tenders and replacement orders.
Do your noise barriers meet EN 14388 and ASTM E90 standards?
All Yukings acoustic barriers are independently tested to EN 14388, ASTM E90, GB/T 19889 and DIN 52210. We provide Rw 25-40 dB airborne sound insulation ratings, EN 1793-1 A1-A4 absorption class certificates, salt-spray test reports and full material certificates (MTC) with every shipment.
Can you provide OEM custom colors and shapes for the noise barrier?
Yes. Yukings runs in-house RAL color matching, custom logo printing, anti-graffiti coatings and curved / T-shape / L-shape profile forming. We support OEM and ODM orders from 500 m² with full confidentiality. For solar-integrated noise barrier projects, we can co-engineer the PV frame with your renewable energy EPC.
Do you offer on-site installation supervision for export projects?
Yes. For orders above 5,000 m², Yukings dispatches an experienced installation supervisor to your site for 7-15 days. The supervisor trains your local crew on HEB post alignment, panel hoisting, rubber gasket seating and torque-controlled fastening, all to BS 5975 / OSHA 1926 standards.
