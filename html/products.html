Product FAQ

Frequently asked noise barrier questions

Answers to the questions our specifiers and procurement teams ask most often when evaluating sound barrier products.

What types of noise barrier does Yukings manufacture?
Yukings manufactures five core noise barrier product lines: railway noise barrier, highway noise barrier, industrial noise control, residential noise barrier and solar noise barrier. Each line is available in galvanized steel, aluminum, reinforced concrete and PC transparent face sheets. We can also mix materials in a single project — for example concrete lower panels with transparent upper panels on a residential highway corridor.
Which materials are available for Yukings sound barriers?
All Yukings sound barrier products can be produced in hot-dip galvanized steel (ISO 1461, 1,500 h salt-spray), aluminum 5052/6061 (perforated and corrugated), reinforced concrete (NCHRP 350 crash-rated) and 20 mm PC transparent panels. Custom RAL colors and wood-grain print are available from 500 m².
What acoustic ratings do Yukings noise barriers achieve?
Yukings noise barrier panels deliver Rw 25-40 dB airborne sound insulation per EN ISO 717, and A1-A4 absorption class per EN 1793-1. Panels are independently tested to EN 14388, ASTM E90, GB/T 19889 and DIN 52210. Test reports and MTC certificates ship with every order.
What is the minimum order quantity for OEM noise barriers?
OEM/ODM orders start from 500 m² for custom colors, profiles or anti-graffiti coating. Standard galvanized steel and aluminum noise barrier SKUs are available from 100 m². A 40HQ container holds roughly 1,200-1,800 m² of panels depending on thickness.
Do you provide installation hardware and posts with the panels?
Yes. Every noise barrier shipment includes the HEB / IPE steel posts, base plates, anchor bolts, EPDM gaskets, self-tapping screws and torque specifications. Optional: cable trays, drainage caps, anti-climb top sections and life-line anchor points. We also dispatch an installation supervisor to your site for orders above 5,000 m².
