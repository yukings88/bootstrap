Our Story

From a Shenzhen workshop
to a global noise barrier maker.

Yukings started in 2006 as a 6-person roll-forming workshop in Shenzhen. The first sound barrier order — 800 m² of galvanized steel panels for a Guangzhou expressway — shipped in 2007. By 2014 we had opened our hot-dip galvanizing line; by 2018 our first EN 14388 railway tender; by 2021 the first PV-integrated noise barrier for an Australian motorway.

  1. 2006 · Founded

    Roll-forming workshop in Shenzhen, 6 employees, one production line.

  2. 2014 · Hot-dip galvanizing

    Opened in-house HDG line, ISO 1461 certified, 1,500 h salt-spray tested.

  3. 2018 · EN 14388 rail tender

    First high-speed rail noise barrier order for Indonesia, 36,000 m².

  4. 2021 · Solar-noise wall

    First PV-integrated noise barrier for Australian M1 motorway, 2.4 MWp.

  5. 2026 · 1,200+ projects

    60+ countries, 5 product lines, 380+ employees, 3.6M m² annual capacity.
