Product Overview

Yukings Galvanized Steel Noise Barriers

What is it

Hot-dip galvanized DX51D+Z steel noise barriers for durable noise control.

Yukings galvanized steel noise barriers combine a hot-dip zinc-coated DX51D+Z louvered or perforated steel face with a high-density hydrophobic mineral-wool core, delivering robust road-traffic and industrial noise absorption with a maintenance-free zinc finish. Panels mount on standard hot-dip galvanized steel H-beam posts with pre-drilled fastener locations.

  • Highway and expressway noise walls
  • Railway and metro sound barriers
  • Industrial plant boundary walls and machinery enclosures
  • Bridge parapets, toll plazas and interchange noise control

Frequently Asked

FAQs — Galvanized Steel Noise Barriers

What steel grade is used for Yukings galvanized steel noise barriers?
Yukings uses DX51D+Z hot-dip galvanized sheet steel (1.0-2.0 mm) for panel face and back sheets, zinc-coated per EN ISO 1461 with an ISO 12944 C3-M / C4-H corrosion rating.
What insertion loss performance can I expect from a galvanized steel barrier?
A standard 100 mm deep galvanized steel absorptive panel delivers 25-40 dB insertion loss (DL) and 30-48 dB sound insulation (Rw), depending on panel depth, mineral-wool density and perforation pattern selected.
Can galvanized steel panels be supplied with a color powder coat?
Yes. Yukings offers optional RAL color powder coating over the hot-dip galvanized base for projects requiring visual harmonization with landscape or infrastructure design guidelines.
What is the design service life and warranty?
Galvanized steel noise barriers are designed for a 25-year service life with a 15-year structural warranty on Yukings-manufactured panels and posts when installed per our engineering guidelines.
Are transparent window sections compatible with galvanized steel barrier posts?
Yes. 8/10/12 mm UV-stabilized polycarbonate transparent panels can be stacked within the same galvanized steel H-post bay using the standard extruded aluminum or folded-steel gasket channel.
