Understanding Noise Impact

🏠

Residential

Traffic and neighborhood noise disrupt sleep, affect mental health, and reduce property values. Peaceful homes are essential for well-being.

🎓

Schools

Excessive noise impairs learning, speech intelligibility, and academic performance. Quiet environments are critical for education.

🏥

Hospitals

Noise disrupts patient recovery, increases stress, and affects medical staff concentration. Healing requires quiet environments.

Frequently Asked Questions

What noise standards apply to hospitals?

Hospitals typically require <40 dB(A) in patient rooms. Our acoustic solutions for healthcare meet WHO guidelines and local standards in USA, Canada, Germany. We work alongside industrial noise specialists on complex projects.

Can you customize solutions for schools?

Absolutely! We design tailored acoustic solutions for classrooms, playgrounds, and school perimeters. Our approach considers safety, supervision lines of sight, and educational requirements while integrating products like transparent acoustic barriers where appropriate.

What is the recommended noise level for residences?

WHO recommends <55 dB(A) during the day and <40 dB(A) at night for residential areas. Our residential noise reduction solutions help achieve these levels through a combination of noise barriers and building treatments, similar to our approach in highway noise projects.

How long does a typical installation take?

Project timeline varies by scope. Small residential projects take 1-2 weeks while larger school or hospital installations may require 4-8 weeks. We work carefully to minimize disruption to daily activities.
