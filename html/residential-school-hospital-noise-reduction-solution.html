Urban Noise Pain Points

Why Civilian Noise Reduction Cannot Be Ignored

According to WHO Environmental Noise Guidelines for the European Region (2018), traffic noise contributes to 18% of population sleep disturbance and 8% hypertension incidence. Yukings civilian noise reduction solution addresses three core civilian zones with dedicated engineering logic.

55-75dB

Residential Compounds

Road traffic noise at typical 4-6 lane urban arterial reaches 70-75dB(A) daytime, well above the WHO 55dB(A) comfort limit. Chronic exposure increases cardiovascular risk and degrades sleep quality. Yukings barrier targets 18-22dB(A) reduction to bring outdoor resting area below 55dB(A).

45-65dB

School & Campus

Classroom noise above 50dB(A) reduces student reading comprehension by 6-9% (WHO 2018). Yukings school barrier focuses on the playground edge and main building facade, combining perimeter barrier with balcony acoustic screen to bring indoor noise below 40dB(A).

40-50dB

Hospital & Elder-care

Hospital ward noise above 45dB(A) night-time extends patient recovery time by 15-20% (Journal of the Acoustical Society of America). Yukings hospital barrier uses anti-bacterial powder coating and low-frequency damped panel to protect patient rest and recovery.

Differentiated Engineering

Three Scenarios, Three Acoustic Logics

Unlike a highway noise barrier, civilian noise control requires scenario-specific design. Yukings engineering team develops dedicated barrier system for each of the three civilian zones to balance acoustic, aesthetic, fire and green-building requirements.

Residential Compound — Privacy + Ventilation

Barrier must reduce traffic noise while preserving natural ventilation and avoiding visual oppression. Yukings solution uses 3-4m height perforated aluminum panel + 1m transparent PC top section, light transmission 80% for upper half, STC 32 for solid lower half. Combined insertion loss 20-22dB(A).

Reference: GB 50118-2010 Civil building sound insulation design code, EN 1793-2 road traffic noise reducing devices.

School & Campus — Safety + Educational Aesthetics

School barriers must be child-safe (rounded corners, anti-climb design) and visually integrated with campus architecture. Yukings solution uses 2.5-3.5m height aluminum frame + 50mm rock wool panel, RAL color customizable to school identity, anti-graffiti coating, fire class A1 non-combustible.

Reference: GB 50099-2011 Code for design of primary and secondary schools, ANSI S12.60 classroom acoustics.

Hospital & Elder-care — Hygiene + Low Frequency

Hospital barrier requires anti-bacterial surface, easy-clean coating, and effective low-frequency dampening (heavy truck rumble). Yukings solution uses 3-4m height galvanized steel + 80mm high-density rock wool, anti-bacterial PVDF coating, STC 36-38 with 28dB(A) low-frequency insertion loss at 125Hz.

Reference: GB 51039-2014 Code for design of general hospital, WHO Environmental Noise Guidelines 2018.

System Architecture

Yukings 4-Layer Civilian Noise Reduction Architecture

L1

Source Control

Quiet pavement (porous asphalt), traffic signal retiming, electric vehicle lane — engineered to reduce noise at source.

L2

Path Barrier

Yukings 3-5m perimeter barrier along community edge, 18-22dB(A) insertion loss, RAL color matching.

L3

Receiver Protection

Building facade upgrade, sound-insulation window STC 40+, acoustic balcony screen for sensitive rooms.

L4

Green Buffer

5-10m wide dense vegetation belt, additional 3-5dB(A) high-frequency attenuation, ecological value.

This 4-layer architecture has been deployed by Yukings across 60+ civilian noise control projects in China, Germany and Australia, achieving an average 23dB(A) on-site composite reduction. The system is OEM-customizable for green-building certification, including LEED v4.1 and China 3-Star.

Zoning Strategy

Perimeter, Façade, Balcony — Three-Zone Treatment

Yukings designs the civilian noise control layout in three concentric zones, each receiving dedicated barrier type and material selection. The perimeter zone uses 3-5m heavy aluminum / steel barrier with rock wool core. The facade zone targets upper floors via 1-2m parapet extension and balcony screen. The window zone recommends STC 40+ sound-insulation window for bedroom and classroom.

  • ✓ Zone 1 Perimeter: full-height barrier 3-5m
  • ✓ Zone 2 Façade: parapet cap + balcony screen 1-2m
  • ✓ Zone 3 Window: STC 40-45 laminated glass
  • ✓ Combined reduction 18-26dB(A) per scenario

FAQ

Civilian Noise Reduction Solution FAQs

What is the recommended noise level limit for residential, school and hospital zones in Yukings solution?

Yukings civilian noise reduction solution targets WHO recommended 55dB(A) daytime / 40dB(A) nighttime for residential, 50dB(A) for school classroom, 45dB(A) night-time for hospital ward. Our barrier system delivers 18-26dB(A) insertion loss to meet these targets per GB 22337 and EN 1793-2.

Does Yukings provide on-site noise survey and acoustic simulation for civilian projects?

Yes. As an OEM solution provider, Yukings engineering team conducts pre-sale noise survey with Type 1 sound level meter, builds 3D CadnaA acoustic model and issues barrier layout plan including height, length, post spacing free of charge for projects above 500m².

Which acoustic barrier material does Yukings recommend for hospital and school environment?

For hospital and school we recommend lightweight aluminum frame + 50mm rock wool composite panel, STC 32-38. Aesthetically neutral RAL 7035 / 9010 color, anti-bacterial powder coating, fire class A1 non-combustible, formaldehyde-free. See our industrial noise control solution for heavy-duty applications.

What is the typical barrier height for residential community facing urban road?

For 4-6 lane urban arterial road, Yukings typically designs 3-4m barrier height at community edge, with 2.5-3m column post spacing. If the road is elevated 4-6m, we extend to 5m barrier with 1m top curved cap to deflect noise upward. Standard wind load 1.0kPa.

Does the residential school hospital noise solution comply with green building standards?

Yes. Yukings civilian solution is designed per LEED v4.1 SS Credit, BREEAM HEA 05, China 3-Star green building label. Material selection prioritizes recyclable aluminum, low-VOC powder coating, EPDM rubber and rock wool with EPD environmental declaration. SGS EPD certificate available.
