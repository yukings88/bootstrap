Certification Overview

Nine industry-recognised standards under one roof

From ISO quality management to IEC photovoltaic testing, Yukings maintains nine active certifications covering raw material sourcing, manufacturing process control, acoustic performance verification and export compliance. Each certification is audited annually by independent bodies.

ISO
9001

ISO 9001:2015 Quality Management System

Yukings quality management system certified to ISO 9001:2015 covering design, manufacture, inspection and after-sales support for noise barrier products.

ISO
14001

ISO 14001:2015 Environmental Management System

Environmental management system for Yukings manufacturing and installation operations, certified ISO 14001:2015 with annual third-party audit.

ISO
45001

ISO 45001:2018 Occupational Health & Safety

Occupational health and safety management system certified ISO 45001:2018 for Yukings noise barrier production and on-site installation operations.

CE

CE Marking per EN 1793 / EN 1794

CE marking with full Technical Construction File (TCF) for Yukings noise barrier products, covering EN 1793 acoustic performance and EN 1794 mechanical durability.

GB/T
34509

GB/T 34509 Chinese National Standard

Yukings noise barrier products meet the Chinese national standard GB/T 34509 for highway noise barrier technical requirements and performance testing.

109
PATENTS

109 Noise Barrier Related Patents

109 independent noise barrier related patents covering acoustic panel design, structural engineering, installation hardware, manufacturing processes and PV-integrated barrier technologies.

SGS
TEST

SGS Third-Party Acoustic Test Reports

Yukings noise barrier acoustic insertion loss, sound absorption and structural performance verified by SGS independent third-party laboratory testing, with full reports available on project request.

ISO
14064

ISO 14064 Carbon Footprint Verified

Yukings noise barrier production line carbon footprint verified to ISO 14064, supporting ESG procurement, green building certification and low-carbon tender requirements.

IEC
61215

IEC 61215 PV Module Performance

Yukings solar noise barrier PV modules tested to IEC 61215 / IEC 61730 international photovoltaic module standards for long-term outdoor durability and power output stability.
