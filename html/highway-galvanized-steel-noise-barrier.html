Material & Process Breakdown

Galvanized Steel Panel

1.0-2.0mm thick galvanized steel sheet with perforated surface, forming acoustic resonance chamber for effective noise absorption.

Rock Wool Core

80-100kg/m³ density mineral wool filling, providing excellent sound absorption across all frequencies.

Steel H-Column

Hot-rolled H-beam with hot-dip galvanization, ensuring structural stability for highway installations.
