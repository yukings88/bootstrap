Frequently Asked Questions

What are OSHA noise exposure limits?

OSHA mandates 90 dB(A) for 8-hour exposure with hearing conservation program required at 85 dB(A). Our industrial noise control systems are designed to meet these standards and those in USA, Canada, Germany. We often work alongside civilian acoustic projects in mixed-use areas.

Can you install during production?

Yes, we provide phased installation with minimal production downtime, working around your schedule. Our modular systems like industrial noise barriers allow flexible installation timing similar to our work on highway construction projects.

What about high-temperature environments?

We offer specialized heat-resistant acoustic materials rated for extreme industrial temperatures. Our engineers select appropriate materials based on your specific operating conditions, and can integrate specialized panel systems where visibility is needed.

Do you provide ongoing monitoring?

We offer both one-time compliance testing and optional continuous noise monitoring systems with real-time alerts for ongoing compliance management.
