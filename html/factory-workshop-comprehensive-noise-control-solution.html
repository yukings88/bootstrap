Industrial Noise Source

Factory Equipment Noise Source Breakdown

Industrial workshops typically contain 5-30 noise sources, each contributing 85-110dB(A) at operator position. Yukings engineering first identifies each source's frequency signature, then designs targeted enclosure, silencer and damping treatment per ISO 9613-2.

95-110dB

Stamping / Punch Press

Low-frequency 125-500Hz impact noise, 1-2Hz repetition, requires damping mass enclosure.

90-100dB

Air Compressor

Broadband 250-4000Hz + 50/60Hz motor hum, intake/exhaust silencer mandatory.

95-105dB

Grinder / Sander

High-frequency 2000-8000Hz abrasive noise, requires absorption-dominant enclosure.

85-100dB

Industrial Fan / Blower

Tonal 500-2000Hz aerodynamic noise, duct silencer & acoustic lag treatment.

100-110dB

Vibration Test Rig

Low-frequency 31-125Hz structural noise, requires floating floor & spring isolator.

Technical Logic

3P Industrial Noise Control: Absorption + Insulation + Damping

A

Sound Absorption (NRC 0.85-1.10)

Yukings 50-100mm high-density rock wool with perforated metal facing absorbs 85-110% of mid-high frequency noise (250-4000Hz), reducing workshop reverberation time from 4-6s to 1-1.5s. NRC 0.85-1.10 per ASTM C423.

I

Sound Insulation (STC 38-52)

Galvanized steel + rock wool composite panel reaches STC 38-52 per ASTM E90, blocking noise transmission through walls, enclosures and barriers. Double-wall construction with air gap achieves STC 50+ for heavy stamping workshop.

D

Vibration Damping (Loss Factor 0.18)

Constrained layer damping treatment with 2mm viscoelastic polymer between steel plates reduces structural vibration 12-18dB. Combined with spring isolators and floating floor, low-frequency 31-125Hz noise is suppressed 15-22dB.

Zoning Strategy

3-Zone Industrial Noise Control Architecture

Yukings designs factory workshop noise control in three concentric zones, each with dedicated engineering logic. This zoning approach delivers 25-35dB(A) total reduction while keeping construction cost 30% lower than single-source enclosure alone.

Z1

Equipment-Level Enclosure (Source Control)

Custom-fit acoustic enclosure around each high-noise equipment (press, compressor, grinder). 80-100mm rock wool composite panel, observation window, ventilation silencer, quick-release maintenance door. Delivers 18-25dB(A) insertion loss at 1m. Compatible with galvanized steel structure for outdoor equipment.

Z2

Workshop-Level Barrier (Path Control)

Ceiling-hung acoustic baffle and partial-height barrier between equipment groups. 50mm rock wool, NRC 0.95. Reduces cross-talk between work stations, lowers ambient noise 8-12dB(A) at operator ear. Pairs with our civilian noise reduction for staff office partitions.

Z3

Plant-Level Perimeter Barrier (Receiver Control)

5-8m tall perimeter barrier along plant boundary, galvanized steel + 100mm rock wool, STC 42, 1.2kPa wind load. Brings plant boundary noise below 65dB(A) daytime, compliant with GB 12348-2008 industrial enterprise boundary noise standard.

FAQ

Factory Workshop Noise Control FAQs

What is the typical noise level of factory workshop before Yukings noise control intervention?

Industrial workshop noise ranges from 85-110dB(A) at operator position: stamping press 95-105dB(A), air compressor 90-100dB(A), punch 100-110dB(A), grinder 95-100dB(A). Yukings engineering target is to reduce operator position below 80dB(A) per OSHA 29 CFR 1910.95 and EU Directive 2003/10/EC.

Which anti-corrosion and heat-resistant material does Yukings use for industrial noise enclosure?

Yukings industrial acoustic enclosure uses hot-dip galvanized steel per ASTM A653 G90, optional 304 stainless steel skin, and high-density 80-100mm rock wool infill rated continuous service temperature 750°C. Optional ceramic fiber blanket for furnace area up to 1050°C service. Similar to our USA coastal highway case ASTM material spec.

Can Yukings design zoning noise control plan for large factory with 20+ equipment?

Yes. Yukings engineering team conducts on-site noise mapping with Type 1 sound level meter, builds 3D EASE acoustic model and delivers zoning plan: equipment-level enclosure, workshop-level barrier, plant-level perimeter barrier, achieving 25-35dB(A) total reduction.

What is the standard factory workshop noise control acceptance threshold in Yukings project?

Yukings delivers projects meeting GBZ 2.2-2007 China industrial noise exposure (8h equivalent 85dB(A)), OSHA 29 CFR 1910.95 (90dB(A) PEL), EU Directive 2003/10/EC (87dB(A) with hearing protection exemption). Third-party SGS / TUV dB test report issued at handover. Compatible with our civilian solution for staff office zones.

Does Yukings factory noise control enclosure interfere with equipment operation, maintenance and ventilation?

No. Yukings enclosure is designed with quick-release panels for maintenance access, integrated ventilation silencer (-25dB(A) at 5m), cable gland sealing, observation window 6mm tempered glass, dust-extraction port and emergency interlock. Modular design allows crane-free assembly in 2-3 days per unit.
