Railway noise barrier solutions for high-speed & metro corridors

Wheel-rail interaction on high-speed rail, freight and metro lines generates low-frequency noise that propagates over long distances. Yukings designs railway noise barrier systems tuned to 63 – 500 Hz, with curved-top or T-shaped diffracting edges that lift insertion loss by 2 – 3 dB compared to standard vertical walls.

Our Engineering Approach

Why our noise barrier solutions perform on site

A barrier that looks correct on a 2D drawing can fail in the field if acoustic, structural and environmental factors aren't considered together. Yukings' engineering workflow closes that loop — from the first dB(A) number to the last torque-wrenched bolt.

  1. Acoustic Simulation, Not Guesswork

    Every solution is modeled in CadnaA or SoundPLAN with terrain, source height and receiver coordinates — not estimated from generic tables.

  2. Wind & Seismic Co-Design

    Posts and foundations are sized to local wind zone (ASCE 7 / EN 1991) and seismic category. We have supplied solutions for typhoon coastal zones and Zone-4 earthquake regions.

  3. Material Pairing for Longevity

    Galvanizing grade, coating system and fastener steel are matched to environment — coastal HDG 600 g/m² + powder, desert PVDF, industrial standard zinc.

  4. Installability by Local Crews

    Panels are sized to standard container fits and designed to be installed with hand tools. Manual is bilingual, with isometric drawings — no Yukings specialist required.

FAQ

Questions buyers ask before they sign a noise barrier PO

The questions our sales engineers hear every week — answered with the same clarity we bring to acoustic modeling.

Ask a Different Question →
What noise reduction solutions does Yukings provide?

Yukings delivers five core noise reduction solutions: railway noise barriers (8 – 12 dB(A) reduction), highway noise control (10 – 15 dB(A)), industrial factory noise barriers, residential acoustic barrier systems, and combined solar noise barrier structures. All are CE, ISO 9001 and EN 14388 compliant.

Which standards do your noise barrier solutions comply with?

Our engineered solutions meet EN 14388 (road traffic noise reducing devices), ASTM E90 / E413 (American sound transmission), GB/T 19889 (Chinese acoustics) and ISO 9001:2015 (quality). Test reports and certificates are available on request.

What is the typical project lead time?

For a standard solution, design approval takes 15 – 25 working days and production 25 – 40 days, plus shipping. Custom engineered solutions with site-specific modeling typically require 35 – 50 days. We support FOB, CIF and DDP terms.

Do you offer on-site installation supervision?

Yes. Every solution ships with full installation documentation. For projects above 5,000 m², we can dispatch a senior engineer (e.g. MR. Yu) to the site for 7 – 15 days. Supervisor flight, accommodation and daily fee are quoted separately.

Can you deliver solutions for desert or coastal environments?

Absolutely. Coastal projects within 1 km of saltwater use HDG 600 g/m² + optional powder coating (80 – 120 μm). Desert / high-UV projects use PVDF coating, UV-resistant PC panels and 316 stainless fasteners. We have delivered to Saudi Arabia, UAE, Australia, Egypt and Indonesia.

How does Yukings begin acoustic engineering for a new project?

Every solution begins with a free feasibility review: source noise level, receiver coordinates, terrain profile and applicable regulation. We then run CadnaA or SoundPLAN modeling, finalize panel height / length, and deliver a sealed engineering report within 5 – 7 working days.
