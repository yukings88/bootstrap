Product Overview

Yukings Solar PV Noise Barriers

What is it

Acoustic barrier + bifacial PV in a single engineered structure.

Yukings solar photovoltaic noise barriers pair a high-performance absorptive or hybrid acoustic panel structure (80-140 mm depth, 25-42 dB insertion loss) with bifacial monocrystalline silicon PV modules mounted on the road-facing or rear face. The integrated system delivers simultaneous road-traffic noise reduction for receivers and renewable electricity generation feeding into grid-tie inverters and medium-voltage grid connections. Built to T/CIET group standard for road traffic photovoltaic noise barrier power generation engineering with full project-specific system design.

Frequently Asked

Solar PV Noise Barriers — FAQs

How much electricity does a solar PV noise barrier generate?

Power generation depends on module wattage (420-540 W standard), barrier length, local latitude/irradiance and orientation. As a guideline each kilometre of Yukings solar PV barrier generates significant renewable megawatt-hours annually — we provide detailed PV energy yield simulation for every project based on your site geographic data, including bifacial albedo-gain estimates.

Does the PV module layer reduce the acoustic performance of the barrier?

No. The bifacial PV modules are mounted on dedicated structural rails separate from the acoustic panel layer. The acoustic barrier itself (80-140 mm absorptive or hybrid panel) maintains its full EN 1793-3 acoustic performance of 25-42 dB insertion loss independently of the PV system. The complete structure is tested to ensure acoustic integrity is not compromised.

What warranties and power performance guarantees are available?

Yukings solar PV noise barriers come with a 25-year linear PV power warranty guaranteeing minimum 80% rated power output at year 25. On the Yukings-supplied structural elements (H-posts, panel frames, mounting rails) we provide a 15-year structural warranty. The complete acoustic structure has a 25-year design service life consistent with our standard noise barrier products.

What engineering documentation and compliance standards apply?

Yukings solar PV noise barriers are engineered in compliance with the T/CIET group standard for road traffic photovoltaic noise barrier power generation engineering. Additional documentation includes full structural wind-load analysis to EN 1991-1-4, complete EN 1793-3 acoustic performance dossier, PV system single-line diagrams, medium-voltage (MV) grid connection specifications and commissioning checklists for turnkey handover.
