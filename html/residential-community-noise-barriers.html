How it works

Visually open absorptive + transparent residential acoustic panels tailored for community environments.

Residential communities need noise control that protects living spaces without creating a walled-in feeling. Yukings residential noise barriers combine a lower absorptive panel section with an upper transparent polycarbonate or cast acrylic window to preserve natural light and visual openness, while custom RAL colors, wood-grain or stone-effect film finishes and green-wall vertical garden integration help the barrier blend smoothly into residential architecture.

Frequently Asked Questions

FAQ — Residential & Community Noise Barriers

Answers to the most common project questions about Yukings noise barriers, from acoustic performance to installation and certification.

How high does a residential noise barrier need to be?
Yukings residential noise barriers are typically 1.5 to 3.5 m tall depending on traffic volume, speed, distance to your building and the specific noise reduction target of your project.
Can residential noise barriers preserve natural light and views?
Yes. Yukings residential noise barriers typically combine a lower absorptive panel section with an upper 8/10/12 mm polycarbonate or cast acrylic transparent window to preserve natural light and visual openness.
Can I match the barrier color to my community design standards?
Yes — Yukings offers full custom RAL powder-coat color matching plus optional wood-grain and stone-effect film finishes to blend with surrounding residential architecture.
Are green-wall or vertical garden residential barrier options available?
Yes. Yukings residential noise barriers can integrate green-wall vertical garden panel sections with planter boxes to support vegetation and create a softer roadside profile.
