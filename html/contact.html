Contact FAQ

Questions about contacting Yukings

Answers to the questions our noise barrier buyers ask most before sending the first email.

How quickly does Yukings respond to a noise barrier inquiry?
We respond to all qualified noise barrier inquiries within 24 hours, Monday through Saturday. Detailed technical quotations with FOB/CIF pricing, lead time and reference projects are sent within 48 hours of receiving your drawings or specification. Mark your subject line [URGENT] for tenders, and we will reply within 2 hours during Beijing business hours.
Can I visit the Yukings factory before placing an order?
Yes. We arrange factory visits Monday-Saturday with airport pickup, on-site hotel booking and full English-Mandarin translation. Most visitors spend 2 days on the production line and 1 day in the QA lab. Please contact Miss Tang at least 14 days in advance so we can align your visit with production cycles.
Which languages does Yukings sales team speak?
Our sales team works in English and Mandarin for written communication, and English/Mandarin for verbal meetings. For technical telephone support, we can also arrange Indonesian and Arabic speaking team members on request. Written quotations are sent in English by default, with Mandarin available on request.
Is NDA available before sharing project details?
Yes. Yukings signs mutual NDA before any reference project data, client name, drawings or proprietary specification is exchanged. We use a standard one-page NDA that can be reviewed, signed digitally and returned within 24 hours. Please mention NDA in your first email to sales.
Do you have a local agent or distributor in my country?
Yukings sells factory-direct to most export markets to keep pricing competitive. We have appointed agents in Indonesia, Saudi Arabia and Germany for in-country technical support. For other markets, our sales manager Miss Tang handles all communication directly from Shenzhen with English/Chinese documentation.
