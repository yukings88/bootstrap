Frequently Asked Questions

Railway noise barrier FAQ from our clients

Answers to the questions we hear most often from railway authorities, EPC contractors and acoustic consultants. Contact our engineering team for project-specific advice.

What noise reduction do Yukings railway noise barriers achieve?
Yukings railway noise barriers deliver Rw 32-40 dB airborne sound insulation per EN ISO 717-1, with A3-A4 absorption class per EN 1793-1. Each configuration is independently tested to EN 14388 by third-party European acoustic laboratories including Fraunhofer IBP and CSTB. Test reports and Mill Test Certificates ship with every order.
Which post systems are used for high-speed rail acoustic barriers?
Our standard railway noise barrier post system uses HEB 100, 140, 160 or 200 sections depending on the wall height (3.0-7.0 m). Posts are hot-dip galvanized per ISO 1461 (85 μm zinc), with torque-bolted connectors, and optional cast-in-place concrete foundation blocks or mechanical anchor bolt solutions for viaduct decks.
Can Yukings supply railway noise wall projects with metro tunnel exits?
Yes. Yukings designs and supplies noise walls for high-speed rail viaducts, metro tunnel exits, freight corridors and station perimeter walls. We work with civil engineering firms on BIM-level coordination for complex transitions, curves (down to 15 m radius), and gradient-connecting details. Hybrid PC transparent + aluminum panels are common for metro above-ground sections.
What is the minimum order quantity for railway noise barriers?
Standard railway noise barrier orders start from 500 m2. OEM/custom profile or custom RAL color configurations start from 1,000 m2. A typical 40HQ container holds approximately 1,200-1,800 m2 of finished panels depending on thickness (80-160 mm). We offer EXW, FOB, CIF and DDP delivery terms.
Do you provide third-party testing certificates for railway noise barriers?
Yes. Every Yukings railway noise barrier shipment ships with the following documents: EN 14388 acoustic test report, EN ISO 717-1 Rw values, ISO 1461 hot-dip galvanizing certificate, mill test certificates (MTC) for steel and aluminum, installation manual, maintenance guide and CE declaration of performance (DoP). UKCA, TUV and GCC GSO certificates available on request.
What is the typical lead time for a 10,000 m2 railway noise barrier project?
A 10,000 m2 railway noise barrier order ships in 25-30 days for standard galvanized steel configurations, or 30-35 days for custom aluminum with powder coat. Yukings operates 3 shifts where required, and has previously delivered 45,000 m2 in a single 45-day window for a high-speed rail viaduct project.
Do Yukings railway noise barriers include drainage and anti-climb details?
Yes. Every Yukings railway noise barrier panel system includes: (1) integrated top-cap drainage profile with EPDM gasket sealing, (2) anti-climb top rail with inward-angled lip, (3) side trim pieces to close joints and (4) bottom-rail rubber seal to stop road debris and water infiltration. All details are shown in our installation and drawing packs.
