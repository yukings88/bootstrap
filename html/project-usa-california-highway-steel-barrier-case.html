Project Overview

The Challenge

An 8-lane urban freeway expansion in Riverside County, California created significant noise impacts for adjacent residential communities, schools, and a local hospital. Pre-construction noise monitoring registered peak levels of 78 dB(A) at 50 feet from the roadway, exceeding local noise ordinances by 13 dB(A).

California DOT (Caltrans) required a solution that met ASTM E1419 standards, preserved sightlines for drivers with strategic transparent sections using PC transparent panels, and integrated aesthetically with the surrounding environment.

Our Solution

Yukings designed a hybrid noise barrier system combining 85% galvanized steel absorptive panels with 15% transparent polycarbonate sections at key interchange locations to maintain driver visibility. The barrier was engineered to 5 meters height with our proprietary galvanized steel noise barrier technology.

The project phased installation during nighttime hours to minimize traffic disruption, with full completion achieved in 60 days including final acoustic testing and verification.
