Geographic & Environmental Context

San Diego County sits on the Pacific coastal plain, with prevailing westerly wind carrying marine salt spray. The project corridor passes within 8 km of the Pacific Ocean, exposing the barrier to high salinity air year-round. Average ambient temperature 18-24°C, occasional heat waves to 38°C. UV index 9-11 in summer — among the highest in continental USA.

Yukings engineering team selected ASTM A653 G90 hot-dip galvanized steel with optional PVDF topcoat, validated through 1,000h ASTM B117 neutral salt spray test and 2,000h QUV-A accelerated UV aging. The barrier system is engineered for 30-year service life in this C4 marine atmospheric corrosivity environment per ISO 12944.

On-site Challenge

The 78dB Coastal Highway Noise Problem

Resident Noise Complaint

Carmel Valley and Mira Mesa communities had filed 240+ formal complaints to Caltrans between 2019-2023. Peak hour traffic noise reached 78dB(A) at front-yard setback, exceeding Caltrans Noise Abatement Criterion 67dB(A) for residential Category B.

Coastal Salt Spray Corrosion

Within 8 km of Pacific Ocean, ASTM B117 salt spray risk is 3-5x higher than inland. Standard G60 galvanizing fails in 8-12 years. Yukings solution upgraded to G90 (275g/m²) + PVDF topcoat for 30-year service.

Seismic Zone D Requirement

San Diego sits in Seismic Zone D per California Building Code, peak ground acceleration 0.4g. Yukings column post HEB240 with reinforced base plate and 4x M30 anchor bolts engineered to IBC and AASHTO LRFD seismic load combination.

Wildlife Corridor Crossing

The I-15 corridor crosses two regional wildlife corridors (coyote, mule deer, San Diego kangaroo rat). Yukings engineered 4 wildlife underpass integration points and 15cm ground clearance to maintain small-mammal passage.

Custom Engineering

Yukings Custom Solution for Caltrans

Yukings developed a customized barrier system based on our vertical metal highway sound barrier platform, adapted to Caltrans Type II standard, ASTM A653 G90 marine spec and AASHTO LRFD seismic combination.

Panel Specification

  • Panel size: 2960mm × 5000mm × 100mm (9.71ft × 16.40ft × 3.94in)
  • Front face: 0.8mm ASTM A653 G90 galvanized steel
  • Rock wool: 50mm 32kg/m³ A1 non-combustible
  • Back face: 0.7mm perforated G90 steel, φ3.0mm hole 23%
  • Topcoat: PVDF 25μm RAL 7035 light grey

Column & Foundation

  • Column: HEB240 Q355B hot-dip galvanized
  • Post spacing: 2.96m (9.71ft) per panel
  • Base plate: 500×500×25mm G90 galvanized
  • Anchor: 4× M30 × 750mm J-bolt, ASTM F1554 Gr.55
  • Foundation: C30 concrete pier 1.2m × 1.2m × 2.5m

ASTM & AASHTO Compliance Summary

Yukings panel system was third-party tested by SGS North America against ASTM A653 / A653M-23 (steel sheet), ASTM B117-19 (salt spray 1000h), ASTM E90-09 (STC 35-38), AASHTO LRFD-9th (wind 1.5kPa + seismic 0.4g), and Caltrans Type II barrier specification. All tests passed. Engineer-of-Record stamp by California-licensed PE issued with shipment.

SGS North America On-Site Test Summary

SGS acoustical engineers conducted 24-hour continuous dB(A) monitoring at 12 measurement points (4 residential setback positions × 3 time periods). Pre-installation peak 78.3dB(A) dropped to 56.1dB(A) post-installation, insertion loss 22.2dB(A), 30% above Caltrans Type II design target 17dB. STC laboratory test on 3 random panel samples returned 36-38, fully compliant with ASTM E90.
