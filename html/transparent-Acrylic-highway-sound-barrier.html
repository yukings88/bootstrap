Panel Engineering

PMMA Cast Acrylic vs Polycarbonate PC

Yukings provides two transparent panel materials for highway sound barrier applications. Cast PMMA acrylic offers superior optical clarity and UV stability for urban landscape projects. Polycarbonate PC offers 5x higher impact strength for expressway sections with stone-chip and vandalism risk.

Cast PMMA Acrylic Panel

Light transmission 92%, density 1.19g/cm³, service temperature -40 to +80°C, 10-year UV yellowing warranty. Best for: city center elevated highway, scenic area, bridge approach.

Polycarbonate PC Panel

Light transmission 88-90%, density 1.20g/cm³, Izod impact 65-80 kJ/m², UV-co-extrusion layer. Best for: high-traffic expressway, rail corridor, vandalism-prone area.

FAQ

Transparent Acrylic Highway Sound Barrier FAQs

What is the light transmission rate of Yukings transparent acrylic highway sound barrier?

Standard PMMA cast acrylic panel reaches 92% light transmission, polycarbonate PC panel reaches 88-90%. Both are UV-stabilized and yellowing-resistant for 10+ years outdoor service, ideal for preserving urban sightline and driver vision. We also offer tinted versions (blue/green/bronze) for anti-glare requirements.

What is the impact strength difference between acrylic and PC transparent sound barrier?

PMMA cast acrylic delivers 12-15 kJ/m² Izod impact strength (ASTM D256), PC polycarbonate delivers 65-80 kJ/m² — about 5x stronger. For expressway projects with stone-chip risk, PC is recommended; for urban landscape, PMMA is more cost-effective. Compare with our galvanized steel vertical barrier for high-impact zones.

How is the transparent acrylic highway sound barrier installed on existing expressway structure?

Yukings transparent panels slide into a hot-dip galvanized HEB100-160 column post system, with EPDM gasket and 3M VHB structural tape backup. Single panel weight 8-12kg/m² (1.64-2.46 lb/ft²), 30% lighter than equivalent steel acoustic wall, ideal for bridge approach retrofit.

Can Yukings provide custom panel size, color tint and anti-glare surface for OEM project?

Yes. We offer custom panel width 500-2000mm, length up to 6000mm, thickness 10/12/15/20mm. Optional blue/green/bronze tint, anti-glare frosted surface and bird-strike pattern engraving available for OEM brand projects. PE-stamped engineering drawings included.

What is the MOQ, lead time and packaging for transparent acrylic highway sound barriers?

MOQ is 100 square meters (1076 sq.ft). Lead time 15-20 working days. Panels are wrapped with PE protective film, foam corner protector, packed in plywood crate for FCL container shipping worldwide including USA, Canada, Australia, EU. CE ISO9001 certificates and SGS test reports included.
