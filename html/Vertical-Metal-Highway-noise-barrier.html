Material & Craft

Galvanized Steel + Rock Wool Composite Structure

The vertical metal highway sound barrier adopts a three-layer composite structure: front face 0.6-0.8mm galvanized steel sheet, middle 50mm rock wool acoustic infill, back face 0.5-0.7mm perforated galvanized steel. Total panel thickness 80-100mm, weight 18-22kg/m² (3.69-4.51 lb/ft²).

  • Face sheet: Hot-dip galvanized steel, ASTM A653 G90, optional PVDF coating
  • Acoustic infill: 50mm / 80mm rock wool, density 32kg/m³, non-combustible A1 grade
  • Back perforated sheet: 0.5-0.7mm galvanized steel, hole pattern φ3.0mm, 23% open area
  • Column post: HEB160-HEB240 hot-rolled steel, hot-dip galvanized, color customizable

Foundation Pouring

02

Column Installation

03

FAQ

Vertical Metal Highway Sound Barrier FAQs

What is the typical height range of your vertical metal highway sound barriers?

Yukings vertical metal highway sound barriers are manufactured from 2m to 6m (6.56ft to 19.69ft) panel height, with column post spacing adjustable at 2m, 2.5m or 3m intervals to fit local roadway cross-section. Custom height available on request.

Which steel grade and surface treatment do you use for highway noise barrier panels?

Panels are produced from hot-dip galvanized steel sheet conforming to ASTM A653 and EN 10346, zinc coating 275g/m² (G90) on both sides, with optional PVDF powder coating for coastal highway projects. Stainless steel 304 face sheet is also available for extreme marine environments.

What is the wind load and STC rating of the vertical metal highway sound barrier?

Standard panels are engineered to withstand 1.5kPa wind pressure (equivalent to 50m/s typhoon zone). Combined with 50mm rock wool infill, the system achieves STC 32 to STC 38 sound transmission class, reducing traffic noise by 18-22dB on site.

Can Yukings customize panel color, length and column post for OEM projects?

Yes. As an OEM manufacturer we provide RAL color matching, panel length 1960-2960mm, column post HEB160-HEB240 and custom base plate. Engineering drawings and PE stamp can be issued for USA, Canada, Australia, EU buyers. See our civilian noise reduction solution for light-weight custom projects.

What is the MOQ, lead time and shipping term for vertical metal highway sound barriers?

MOQ is 200 square meters (2152 sq.ft). Production lead time is 18-25 working days after deposit. We ship FOB Qingdao, CIF or DDP worldwide with 40HQ container loading of 800-1000 square meters per unit. CE ISO9001 certificates and SGS test reports included.
