Engineering Depth

Four materials.
One certified acoustic barrier system.

Our noise barrier walls are not one product. They are a system of face sheets, absorptive cores, posts and gaskets — each chosen for the project's acoustic target, climate and certification standard.

Galvanized Steel

Hot-dip galvanized to ISO 1461, 1,500 h salt-spray tested. Default for highway and rail.

Aluminum 5052 / 6061

Perforated + corrugated. Rw 32-40 dB. Lightweight, 25-year coastal rating.

Reinforced Concrete

Cast-in-place and precast panels, NCHRP 350 crash-rated base available.

PC Transparent Panel

20 mm polycarbonate sheets, UV-stable, anti-graffiti coated for residential zones.
