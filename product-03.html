FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Why use louver design for highway noise barriers?

Louver design maintains 30%+ open area for natural ventilation while reducing noise 20-30dB. Ideal for tunnels, semi-enclosed sections, and air-quality-sensitive zones where solid barriers would trap exhaust fumes.

What is the open area ratio of louver noise barriers?

Yukings louver barriers offer 30% standard open area ratio, with custom options from 20% to 65%. Higher open area allows more airflow; lower open area provides better noise reduction. We help select the right balance.

Are louver noise barriers suitable for tunnels?

Yes! Louver barriers are specifically designed for tunnel entrances, cut-and-cover sections, and underpasses. They reduce tunnel CO buildup 60% and prevent noise reflection between opposite carriageways.

How are louver noise barriers maintained?

Modular louver panels allow individual removal for cleaning or replacement. Standard pressure washing and galvanizing inspection every 2-3 years maintains 25+ year service life.

What is the minimum order quantity for louver barriers?

Yukings standard MOQ is 100㎡ for louver highway noise barriers. Custom angles or open area ratios may require 200㎡ MOQ. Tunnel retrofit projects enjoy flexible terms.

Can louver barriers be customized for ventilation requirements?

Yes! Yukings engineers CFD airflow models for tunnel projects. Custom louver angles and spacing balance ventilation needs with 20-30dB noise reduction for any tunnel or semi-enclosed section.
