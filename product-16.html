FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a residential community noise barrier?

A residential community noise barrier is a decorative acoustic wall designed for residential streets and community boundaries. Yukings residential barriers deliver 20-30dB reduction with wood-grain and stone-texture finishes plus optional privacy screens.

How much noise do residential barriers reduce?

Yukings residential community barriers achieve 20-30dB reduction, sufficient to bring 65-75dB street traffic noise down to 35-55dB inside homes, restoring sleep quality and conversation comfort.

Do residential barriers provide privacy?

Yes! Yukings residential barriers offer optional privacy screen sections that block 80% of visual sight lines, providing visual privacy for ground-floor living areas and rear gardens.

What color and finish options are available?

Full RAL palette plus premium wood-grain (oak, walnut, teak] and stone-texture (sandstone, slate, granite] PVDF and powder-coat finishes for residential architecture integration.

Are residential barriers eco-friendly?

Yes! Yukings residential barriers are EPD certified with low-VOC powder coating and recyclable aluminum. Compliant with LEED v4 and BREEAM residential standards.

What is the minimum order quantity for residential barriers?

Yukings standard MOQ is 50㎡ for residential community noise barriers. Custom wood-grain or stone-texture finishes may require 100㎡ minimum order quantity.
