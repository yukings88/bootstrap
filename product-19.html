Frequently Asked Questions

What is a custom modular noise barrier system?

A custom modular noise barrier system is a bespoke acoustic solution for non-standard projects. Yukings custom systems deliver any shape, color, size, and material combination with free 3D modeling and design service.

What custom materials are available?

Yukings offers hot-dip galvanized steel, 5052 aluminum, stainless steel 304, FRP cladding, PC transparent, tempered glass, wood-grain composite, and recycled composite — any combination.

What custom shapes are possible?

Any shape is possible: standard flat, curved arc (R150-R800], multi-angle polygon, wave/undulating, tapered height, stepped tier, perforated pattern, or fully custom CAD geometry.

Is the 3D design service really free?

Yes! Free 3D CAD modeling from architectural drawings or site survey. Acoustic simulation, wind load calculation, and structural FEA analysis included at no charge.

What is the minimum order quantity for custom systems?

Yukings custom noise barrier systems start at 100㎡. Smaller custom projects negotiable. Free feasibility study provided for any requirement.

How long does a custom project take?

Yukings custom projects typically take 4-8 weeks from drawing to installation, including 3D modeling, prototype, manufacturing, and on-site installation. 50% faster than industry average 3-6 months.
