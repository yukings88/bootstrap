Installation Process

Metro Viaduct Noise Barrier: Easy Installation & Maintenance

Lightweight aluminum panels install with 2-person crew and no heavy crane. Bolted connections to existing viaduct post anchors.

1
Step 01

Foundation Work

Verify existing viaduct post anchor locations. Drill and epoxy additional anchors if needed for retrofit.

2
Step 02

Isolator & Post Installation

Mount EPDM isolators on existing post anchors. Bolt aluminum H-beam posts to anchors.

3
Step 03

Panel Mounting

Slide aluminum panels between posts using rubber isolation mounts. Lock with stainless fasteners.

4
Step 04

Inspection & Certification

Perform vibration and acoustic testing. Submit metro authority compliance documentation.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a metro viaduct noise barrier?

A metro viaduct noise barrier is a lightweight acoustic wall designed for elevated metro bridge sections. Yukings metro viaduct barriers deliver 25-35dB reduction with 5052-H32 aluminum, reducing deck load 60% versus steel.

How much do metro viaduct barriers weigh?

Yukings metro viaduct barriers weigh 4-6kg/㎡ with 5052-H32 aluminum alloy, 60% lighter than equivalent steel. Reduces bridge deck load by 60%, eliminating reinforcement needs on aging viaducts.

Are metro viaduct barriers resistant to acid rain?

Yes! Yukings PVDF or anodized coating survives 3000+ hours ASTM B117 salt-spray testing, maintaining integrity in urban acid rain (pH 4-5] environments for 25+ years.

What is the minimum order quantity for metro viaduct barriers?

Yukings standard MOQ is 100㎡ for metro viaduct noise barriers. Custom lengths or colors may require 200㎡ minimum order quantity.

Can metro viaduct barriers prevent vibration transfer?

Yes! Yukings EPDM isolators are tuned for 2-8Hz metro train vibration, preventing structure-borne noise amplification and preserving 25-35dB reduction at receiver.

Are metro viaduct barriers suitable for retrofit projects?

Yes! Yukings metro viaduct barriers install on existing viaduct post anchors. Lightweight aluminum eliminates need for deck reinforcement, ideal for retrofit of aging metro bridges.
