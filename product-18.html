FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a hospital quiet zone noise barrier?

A hospital quiet zone noise barrier is a medical-grade acoustic wall designed for hospital perimeters and clinic zones. Yukings hospital barriers deliver 30-40dB reduction with ISO 22196 anti-bacterial coating and MRI-compatible vibration isolators.

How much noise do hospital barriers reduce?

Yukings hospital quiet zone barriers achieve 30-40dB reduction, sufficient to bring 70-80dB traffic noise down to 30-50dB at patient rooms, improving sleep quality and reducing hospital stay duration.

Are hospital barriers anti-bacterial?

Yes! Yukings hospital barriers use ISO 22196 certified anti-bacterial coating achieving 99.9% bacterial reduction on panel surface, meeting hospital hygiene protocols for patient zones.

Are hospital barriers MRI-compatible?

Yes! Yukings hospital barriers use medical-grade vibration isolators that prevent acoustic and structure-borne interference with sensitive MRI, CT, and ultrasound imaging equipment in adjacent zones.

Are hospital barrier coatings non-toxic?

Yes! Yukings hospital barriers use zero-VOC powder coating containing no volatile organic compounds. EPD certified, LEED v4 Healthcare compliant, with Cradle-to-Cradle certified material options.

What is the minimum order quantity for hospital barriers?

Yukings standard MOQ is 100㎡ for hospital quiet zone noise barriers. Custom anti-bacterial coating or MRI-compatible isolators may require 200㎡ minimum order quantity.
