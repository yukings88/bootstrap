FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Why choose aluminum noise barrier over steel?

Yukings aluminum noise barriers offer 60% weight reduction compared to steel, making them ideal for elevated bridges and structures with load limitations. Aluminum also provides superior corrosion resistance, especially in coastal areas.

What is the MOQ for custom colored aluminum barriers?

Yukings standard RAL colors have no MOQ premium. Custom colors require minimum 500㎡. Anodized finishes require 300㎡ minimum for aluminum noise barriers.

Can aluminum noise barriers be used in cold climates?

Yes! Yukings aluminum barriers maintain structural integrity from -40°C to +70°C. Unlike steel, aluminum does not become brittle in extreme cold.

How does aluminum handle thermal expansion?

Yukings designs expansion joints for 2.4mm/meter movement (60°C temperature swing). Sliding connections accommodate thermal movement without stress.

Do you provide engineering design assistance?

Yes! Yukings engineering team provides acoustic modeling, wind load calculations, and CAD drawings for aluminum noise barriers. Sample panels available for testing.
