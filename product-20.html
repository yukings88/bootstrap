Noise Barrier Accessories: Universal Fit Accessories Catalog

Original Yukings-manufactured accessories for noise barrier systems. Universal fit across our full product range, including posts, bolts, gaskets, brackets, drainage, and acoustic infill. Same quality, same warranty.

🔩

Posts & H-Beams

Hot-dip galvanized H-beam posts in 100x100, 150x150, 200x200, 250x250, 300x300mm sizes. Aluminum H-beam options. Custom lengths to 10m. Anti-corrosion coating. Universal fit.

🔧

Bolts, Nuts & Fasteners

Stainless steel A2-70 / A4-80 bolts, M8-M24 sizes. High-strength 8.8/10.9 grade carbon steel options. Security tamper-resistant variants. Galvanized and coated finishes.

🛡️

EPDM Gaskets & Seals

EPDM rubber gaskets for panel joints, perimeter seals, and EPDM isolator mounts. UV-stabilized, ozone-resistant, 25+ year service life. Custom profiles available.

🔗

Brackets & Clamps

Post-to-panel mounting brackets, panel-to-panel clamps, anti-vibration clamps. Hot-dip galvanized steel, stainless steel, and aluminum options. Universal fit.

🌧️

Drainage & Weatherproofing

Integrated drainage gutters, downspouts, weep holes, weather caps. Stainless steel and PVC options. EPDM weather seals. Anti-ponding design.

🔊

Acoustic Infill Materials

Mineral wool (50-128 kg/m³], glass wool, rock wool, and recycled PET acoustic infill. Custom cut to barrier dimensions. Class A fire rated. NRC 0.85-0.95.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Are Yukings accessories universal across barrier systems?

Yes! All Yukings noise barrier accessories are designed to exact tolerances of every barrier system in our catalog. Universal fit with no field modification required.

Do accessories carry the same warranty as main products?

Yes! Original Yukings accessories carry the same 25+ year warranty as the main barrier system. No warranty voiding, no risk of incompatibility.

How fast can I get replacement accessories?

Standard accessories ship within 48 hours from regional warehouses worldwide. Custom accessories typically 2-4 weeks. Emergency expedited shipping available.

What is the minimum order quantity for accessories?

Yukings accessories have no minimum order quantity for stocked items. Custom accessories typically require 100-piece minimum for cost-effective production.

Can I use third-party accessories with Yukings barriers?

Technically yes, but this voids the main barrier warranty. Yukings strongly recommends original accessories to maintain warranty coverage and ensure exact fit, finish, and performance.

Do you provide installation guidance for accessories?

Yes! Yukings provides detailed installation manuals, video tutorials, and on-call engineering support for all accessory categories. Field engineer dispatch available for complex projects.
