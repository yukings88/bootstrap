Installation Process

Railway Station Noise Barrier: Easy Installation & Maintenance

Detailed coordination with rail authority and station operator. Off-peak or night-shift installation to avoid service disruption. Bolted connections, no on-site welding.

1
Step 01

Planning & Permit

Coordinate with rail authority for off-peak installation. Obtain platform closure permit and safety plan.

2
Step 02

Foundation Work

Pour reinforced concrete foundations at 2m intervals during night-shift window. Curing during service hours.

3
Step 03

Post & Panel Installation

Mount H-beam posts with anti-climb caps. Slide acoustic panels between posts. Lock with security fasteners.

4
Step 04

Inspection & Handover

Perform fire, acoustic, and crowd-safety testing. Submit handover documentation to rail authority.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a railway station noise barrier?

A railway station noise barrier is a maximum-attenuation acoustic wall designed for station platforms and approach zones. Yukings station barriers deliver 35-45dB reduction with Class A fire rating and anti-climb design.

How much noise do station barriers reduce?

Yukings station barriers achieve 35-45dB reduction, sufficient to bring 95-110dB train arrival and crowd noise down to 50-65dB at the platform waiting area, improving announcement clarity and passenger comfort.

Are station barriers fire rated?

Yes! All Yukings station barriers meet EN 13501-1 Class A non-combustible rating with s1 low-smoke emission, ensuring passenger safety during emergencies.

What is the minimum order quantity for station barriers?

Yukings standard MOQ is 200㎡ for railway station noise barriers. Station projects typically 1km+. Custom egress or branding may require 500㎡ minimum order quantity.

Can station barriers prevent crowd climbing?

Yes! Yukings station barriers use smooth anti-climb top profile eliminating handholds and footholds, preventing track intrusion. Integrated with crowd control design per rail authority requirements.

Can station barriers integrate emergency egress?

Yes! Yukings station barriers are pre-engineered for integrated panic doors and emergency egress corridors, allowing rapid passenger evacuation in compliance with fire code and rail authority requirements.
