Installation Process

Chemical Plant Noise Barrier: Easy Installation & Maintenance

FRP-clad panels require careful handling to preserve surface integrity. Stainless steel fasteners and FRP-sleeved posts ensure chemical resistance at every joint.

1
Step 01

Foundation Work

Pour reinforced concrete foundations at 2m intervals. Chemical-resistant anchor bolts specified.

2
Step 02

Post Installation

Mount H-beam posts with FRP protective sleeves at the base. Two-person crew with light crane.

3
Step 03

FRP Panel Mounting

Lift FRP-clad panels with crane. Bolt to posts with stainless steel fasteners. Verify panel alignment and surface integrity.

4
Step 04

Inspection & Testing

Perform chemical resistance and acoustic testing. Final inspection verifies FRP integrity and acoustic seals.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a chemical plant noise barrier?

A chemical plant noise barrier is an acid-alkali resistant acoustic wall designed for chemical industrial parks. Yukings chemical plant barriers deliver 30-40dB reduction with FRP coating rated pH 2-12 for corrosive atmospheres.

How resistant are chemical plant barriers to acids?

Yukings chemical plant barriers use FRP lamination rated pH 2-12, surviving 5000+ hours salt spray per ASTM B117. Tested against sulfuric, hydrochloric, nitric, and sodium hydroxide atmospheres.

Can chemical plant barriers withstand solvent vapors?

Yes! Sealed FRP-resin joints and continuous FRP back panels prevent solvent vapor permeation that attacks adhesive bonds in standard panels.

What is the minimum order quantity for chemical plant barriers?

Yukings standard MOQ is 100㎡ for chemical plant noise barriers. Custom FRP formulations may require 200㎡ minimum order quantity.

Are chemical plant barriers suitable for marine and coastal sites?

Yes! The FRP coating passes 5000+ hours ASTM B117 salt-spray testing, making it ideal for chemical plants in marine and coastal industrial zones.

What chemicals can chemical plant barriers resist?

Yukings FRP chemical plant barriers resist sulfuric, hydrochloric, phosphoric, nitric, sodium hydroxide, and most organic solvents. Custom formulations available for specific chemical compatibility.
